Underground Leak Detection

A hidden leak can waste hundreds of gallons a day, inflate your water bill and cause structural damage. Our leak detection uses proven methods to locate underground leaks quickly and precisely — no guessing holes.

What we'll check

1

Valve diagnosis — check for chatter, slow shut-off or leaking valves.

2

Breaks in the line — using pressure tests and isolating areas.

3

Manifold leaks — check for cracks around the valve box.

4

Water-meter validation — check the meter to pinpoint hidden leaks.

Leak Detection FAQs

How do I know if I have a leak?
Soft or soggy spots, water pooling where it shouldn't, unexpectedly high water bills, or a meter that keeps running when everything is off.
Do you repair the leak once you find it?
Yes — we locate the leak, explain the repair, and fix it on the spot when possible so your system is watertight again.
Can leak detection save me money?
Yes — finding a hidden leak early can save hundreds of gallons a day and prevent costly damage to your landscape and foundation.
